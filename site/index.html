Chapter 4

“I have asked the HSE to reply directly”

Many questions are about services run by agencies, not departments. So ministers often pass them on: the HSE, the NTA, the RSA reply to the TD instead. That's normal procedure, not dodging, so we count it separately. But it has become far more common.

The fine print

How far to trust this

The model is right often enough to measure trends across thousands of replies, not to judge any single reply on its own. That's why this page shows departments and trends, and deliberately doesn't rank individual ministers. These numbers are measured, not claimed.

How it works

Collect

Questions and replies come from the Houses of the Oireachtas open data API.

Judge

Each reply goes to Jev (TypeSafe AI), a model that returns decisions with probabilities rather than writing text.

Classify

Categories follow a published taxonomy of answer clarity (Thomas et al., EMNLP 2024). Passing a question on is counted separately.

Update

A scheduled job checks for new replies every day and rebuilds this page. Each reply costs a few thousandths of a cent to judge.
